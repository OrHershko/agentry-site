Agentry privacy policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Agentry does not collect, store or sell personal data on any server operated by its developer. There is no Agentry account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

  • The address of your server, your sign-in session and your settings are stored locally; the session is kept in the iOS Keychain.
  • Cached conversations and drafts are stored locally and are erased when you remove a connection in the app.

What is sent, and where

Agentry connects only to the server address you enter. Your messages, attachments, voice audio and profile settings are sent to that server, which you control. What your server does next (for example calling a model provider) is governed by your server's configuration and that provider's policy, not by Agentry.

Permissions

  • Microphone: voice conversations with your agent.
  • Camera and photo library: attach images you choose to a message.
  • Local network: reach your server on your private network or Tailscale.
  • Notifications: optional alerts from your server, delivered through Apple Push Notification service. The preview text is end-to-end encrypted, so Apple sees only a generic notice.

Children

Agentry is not directed at children.
